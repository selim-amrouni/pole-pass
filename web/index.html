About this demo

I used public Mapillary photos to map poles in {{LOCATION}}, identify visible equipment, and flag things worth checking. The aim is to see what is useful for an initial asset review, and where the photos are insufficient. Open a pole to see the photos and judge the result.

{{VALIDATION_NOTICE}}

How it works

  1. Find poles. Mapillary detections identify candidate poles in public street photos.
  2. Read the photos. A vision model describes visible equipment and flags possible issues.
  3. Check the result. Open each record to review its photos, compare views, and record your assessment.
Technical details{{TECH_DETAILS}}

What the photos cannot establish

Photos cannot establish structural condition, electrical loading, ownership, or compliance. Old images, camera angles, and obstructions can mislead the model. A record with no flag has not been inspected.{{OSM_NOTE}}

Filters and review

One flag filter applies at a time; the photo date, the review status, and the extra options narrow it further. "Reviewed" means every flag on the record has a decision; a note alone does not count. Decisions are saved in this browser only and go into the filtered exports as separate review columns, with unreviewed left blank.
