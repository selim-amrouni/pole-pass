About this demo

This is a small experiment using public street-level imagery in Greenpoint. The aim is to see what is useful for an initial asset review, and where the photos are insufficient.

How it works

Mapillary detections locate candidate poles. A vision model reviews cropped photos for pole type, visible equipment, attachments, and possible condition issues. Nearby detections are grouped into pole records. Where several photos are available, the results are compared.

Technical details{{TECH_DETAILS}}

What the photos cannot establish

These photos do not establish structural condition, electrical loading, attachment ownership, or compliance. Old, blurred, obstructed, or distorted images can give misleading results. A record with no model flag has not been cleared by an inspection.
