How it works

  1. Find poles. Mapillary detections identify candidate poles in public street photos.
  2. Read the photos. A vision model describes visible equipment and flags possible issues.
  3. Check the result. Open each record to review its photos, compare views, and record your assessment.

What it cannot do

Photos cannot establish structural condition, electrical loading, ownership, or compliance. Old images, camera angles, and obstructions can mislead the model. A record with no flag has not been inspected.

Each area page has the technical details: how photos are chosen, how records are merged, how dates are used, and what has and has not been validated. The comparison with OpenStreetMap uses volunteer mapping, not utility inventory.
